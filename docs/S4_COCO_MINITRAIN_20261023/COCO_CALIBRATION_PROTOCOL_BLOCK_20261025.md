The existing serial workflow processed all **25,000** clean COCO training images. Its fixed StrictP5 selector chose **39 of 256** P5 channels, while the original SafeBG consumer requires exactly **24**. The phase recorded `BLOCKED_PROTOCOL_COMPATIBILITY`; the serial controller recorded `FAILED` without retry. The bound controller and calibration child were verified absent after termination.

The result used the frozen StrictP5 thresholds. It reported 78 safe candidates and 103 target candidates. No channel truncation or threshold relaxation was applied. The raw statistics, eligible support manifest, K=8 prototype bank and StrictP5 block receipt remain on the owned server.

The original Person support rule found **13,630** eligible training images and recorded **20,448** derived Fourier steps (`24 × ceil(13,630 / 16)`) for a compatible selection. This does not authorize Fourier with the incompatible 39-channel result. Validation images were not opened; parameter updates, checkpoint selection and official metrics were disabled. No poisoned export, matched Clean/S4 victims, or final S4 evaluation ran.
